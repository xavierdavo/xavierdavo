## Projects and contributions

### [Accessible AI Self-Service Kiosk](https://github.com/xavierdavo/tcc-totem-acessivel)

An academic team project focused on making product search and in-store guidance more accessible, especially for people with visual impairments.

- Combines a web interface, a **Python/FastAPI** backend, product data in **SQLite**, speech recognition, language-model integration, and spoken responses.
- Includes REST endpoints, an illustrative 2D store map, Docker configuration, and backend tests.
- I contributed to project planning and documentation, and made improvements to the product-search and map-confirmation flow.
- Presented as part of the **25th National Congress of Scientific Initiation — CONIC-SEMESP**.

**Explore:** [repository](https://github.com/xavierdavo/tcc-totem-acessivel) · [experimental demo](https://totem-acessiveltcc.netlify.app/)

### [DevOps Platform / Academic Marketplace](https://github.com/GestaoProjetos2026/devops-platform)

**Explore:** [repository](https://github.com/GestaoProjetos2026/devops-platform) · [one of my backend commits](https://github.com/GestaoProjetos2026/devops-platform/commit/ce5984465399d97636061448759e3d6443d10c85)
